Common questions

What's the difference between checking something off and removing it?

Checking the circle means you bought it: it's saved to your purchase history, with the store and time, and moves to the store's Purchased section. Removing an item (swipe left, or touch and hold) just takes it off the list without recording a purchase.

How do I share my list with my household?

Settings → Household → Invite Someone, then send the invitation by Messages, Mail, or any other way. The other person needs an Apple Account signed in to iCloud and Simple Aisle installed; they tap the link and your household appears. Everyone sees the same stores, lists, and purchase history.

Can the same item be on two stores' lists?

Yes. When you add something that's already on another list, Simple Aisle asks whether to add it here too or move it here. After you buy it at one store, it offers to take it off the other list as well. You don't have to decide right away.

How does it decide what to suggest?

Once you've bought something on a few different days, Simple Aisle knows roughly how often you buy it. When that much time has passed, it shows up under Suggested. Frequently Purchased shows what you buy most, whether or not it's due. Nothing is ever added for you.

An item landed in the wrong category.

Tap the item and choose a different category. Simple Aisle remembers that choice for your household and won't change it again. You can rename, recolor, and reorder categories in Settings → Categories & Colors. Their order is the order your lists are grouped in.

I undid something by mistake.

Tap Redo (the arrow next to Undo). On iPad with a keyboard, ⌘Z undoes and ⇧⌘Z redoes.

My lists aren't showing up on my other device.

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud, and that Simple Aisle is allowed to use iCloud (Settings → your name → iCloud). Then open Simple Aisle on both. Changes sync through iCloud, usually within a few seconds when the app is open.
