Privacy Policy

Simple Aisle

Effective 27 September 2026

HomeSupport

The short version. Simple Aisle has no account system and no server of its own. Your lists and purchase history are stored on your devices and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account. If you share your household, the people you invite can see it too. Arosemena Apps never receives your data. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

What is stored

InformationWhy the app has it

Purchase history: which item, which store, and the date and time you checked it offLast purchased dates, suggestions, and statistics
Your household's nameShown in Settings and on sharing invitations
Small preferences, such as which stores are expandedSo the app opens the way you left it (kept on each device only)

Simple Aisle does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not use prices or payment information of any kind.

Where it is stored

  • On your devices. Everything works without a network connection.
  • In your iCloud (optional). When you're signed in to iCloud, Simple Aisle syncs your household through Apple's CloudKit to your other devices. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. Arosemena Apps cannot read it.
  • With the people you invite (optional). If you share your household, the people you invite see and can change the same stores, lists, categories and purchase history. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing, between Apple Accounts. You can remove people or stop sharing at any time in Settings → Household.

So your data does leave your device when you use iCloud sync or sharing. It goes to Apple's iCloud and to the people you choose, and never to Arosemena Apps.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Simple Aisle with Siri or Shortcuts, the item and store names you speak or type are processed by Apple under Apple's terms, then handed to the app on your device.

What Simple Aisle does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting that sends your content, no remote configuration, and no artificial intelligence services. Suggestions and categories are worked out on your device. The app contains no third-party code.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Arosemena Apps receives no payment details. Tips unlock nothing, and the app does not record whether you have tipped.

Deleting your data

Deleting an item that you've bought before hides it and keeps its history, so your statistics stay correct. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. If your household is shared, the other members keep their copy until the owner stops sharing.

Children

Simple Aisle is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the previous version stays in this site's history on GitHub.
