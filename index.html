Simple Aisle

A grocery list that remembers.

Privacy Policy Support

Simple Aisle is a grocery list for your household on iPhone and iPad. Checking something off means "we bought it": the app remembers when and where, so the things you buy all the time are one tap away next time.

How it works

  • Make a list for each store you shop at, with its own color and icon. Items group themselves by category.
  • Open a store while you shop and tap once as things go in the cart. Undo and Redo are always there.
  • Add Item suggests what you're probably running low on, based on how often your household usually buys it.
  • The same item can be on more than one store's list, and Simple Aisle tells you where it already is.
  • Invite your household with iCloud: everyone sees the same lists and purchase history, sees who added what, and can keep a store private.
  • Checked something off by mistake? Fix the date or remove it from the item's history.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "Add to my grocery list in Simple Aisle."

What it does not do

No account. No sign-in. No subscription. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Simple Aisle is free, with optional tips that unlock nothing.
